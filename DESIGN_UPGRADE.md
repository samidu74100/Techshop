## 📱 Responsive Design

### Breakpoints

```css

Tablet:    640-768px (md)
Desktop:   768-1024px (lg)
Large:     1024-1280px (xl)
XLarge:    > 1280px (2xl)
```

### Adaptations

- **Mobile:** Grille 1 colonne, menu hamburger
- **Tablet:** Grille 2 colonnes, nav réduite
- **Desktop:** Grille 3-4 colonnes, nav complète
- **Large:** Grille 4+ colonnes, espacement max
